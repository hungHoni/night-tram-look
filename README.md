# Night Tram, look A

A live 3D look test for Night Tram: a handmade model tram street, shown at the real size of a strip along the bottom of your screen. Use the Light switch for golden hour, morning or night.

This is a look test, not the app. There is no sound yet, and the riders are pretend.

Drawn with three.js (MIT licence).

`speed/`: the Night Tram speed check, for test laptops. It runs the Paris tram strip for about 2 minutes and shows how smoothly it ran, for you to copy and send. Nothing is sent anywhere.

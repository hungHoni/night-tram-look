Night Tram speed check

This checks how smoothly the tram strip above runs on this computer. It takes about 2 minutes.

  1. If you can, plug in the charger and close other apps.
  2. Press Start, then leave the computer alone until the results show. Keep this window in front: don't switch tabs, cover it or resize it.
  3. Press Copy results and paste them in a message to Hung.

Nothing is sent anywhere: the results stay on this page until you copy them.

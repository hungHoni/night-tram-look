3. The harbour in winter

By the time the ferries stopped, the harbour had gone quiet in a way nobody in town could remember. The fish market closed at noon, and the gulls moved inland to the car parks.

On Thursdays my grandmother still walked to the end of the pier, carrying a thermos and a folded newspaper she never opened.
